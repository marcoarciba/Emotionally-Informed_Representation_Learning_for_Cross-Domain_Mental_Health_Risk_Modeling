# Research Hypothesis

Emotionally informed latent representations preserve meaningful semantic, geometric, and topological structures that improve transferability and interpretability across complex human-centered domains.

## Main Hypothesis

Latent spaces enriched with emotional information can provide more robust and transferable representations than purely semantic or task-specific embeddings.

## Expected Benefits

- Improved transfer learning
- Better generalization across domains
- More meaningful latent structures
- Enhanced explainability
- Better modeling of human phenomena
