# Emotionally Informed Representation Learning

Research exploring emotionally informed deep representations for the transferable analysis of complex human phenomena through geometric, topological, and latent-space approaches.

---

## Research Vision

Human behavior emerges from complex interactions between emotions, cognition, context, and social dynamics.

Traditional machine learning systems often focus on observable patterns while ignoring underlying emotional structures that shape decision-making and behavior.

This research investigates how emotionally informed representations can improve the understanding, transferability, and interpretability of complex human phenomena.

## Research Objectives

- Learn emotionally informed latent representations
- Explore semantic and emotional structures in high-dimensional spaces
- Analyze geometric properties of learned embeddings
- Investigate topological characteristics of representation spaces
- Improve transferability across domains and populations
- Support human-centered AI applications

## Research Areas

### Emotional AI

Developing representations that preserve emotionally relevant information.

### Representation Learning

Learning meaningful latent structures from complex and high-dimensional data.

### Geometric Deep Learning

Analyzing the geometric properties of latent spaces and learned embeddings.

### Topological Data Analysis

Studying structural patterns and relationships through topological approaches.

### Transfer Learning & Domain Adaptation

Improving model generalization across different contexts, domains, and populations.

### Mental Health Analytics

Exploring the use of emotionally informed representations for understanding psychological and behavioral patterns.
